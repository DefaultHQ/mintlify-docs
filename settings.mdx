Settings is where you manage your own account and, if you're an admin, configure the whole
workspace. You'll find your personal settings (profile, availability, notifications)
alongside workspace controls (team, branding, integrations, data, scheduler appearance, and
SSO).

<Note>
  Open Settings from the **Dock** — the app bar at the bottom of the screen — by selecting
  the **Settings** app (the gear icon), alongside your other Default apps (Tables, Routing,
  Scheduling, Workflows). Settings opens in its own panel, split into **User settings** and
  **Workspace settings**. Most workspace settings are available to admins.
</Note>

## User settings

### Profile

Your personal details, shown on your booking pages and meeting invites.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Personal details">
    Set your **first** and **last name**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Display name">
    The name people see on your booking pages and calendar invites, such as
    `Brennon from Default`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Job title, phone number, and email">
    Add your **job title** and **phone number**, and review your **email**. Adding a phone
    number also unlocks the **SMS** notification channel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Profile picture">
    Upload an avatar shown across the product and on your booking pages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Availability & OOO

Control when you can be booked.

**Personal preferences**

| Setting | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vacation mode** | Pauses new bookings while you have an active out-of-office block. | Turn it on before time off so no one books you that week. |
| **Pre-meeting buffer** | Minutes blocked before each meeting. | `15 min` for a breather before every call. |
| **Post-meeting buffer** | Minutes blocked after each meeting. | `15 min` to write notes. |
| **Notice period** | Minimum lead time before a slot can be booked. | `4 hours`, so no one books you ten minutes out. |
| **Primary calendar** | The connected calendar new meetings are written to. | Your Google work calendar. |

**Working hours** — Create one or more named schedules of per-day time ranges that define
when you're bookable. For example, a "Default" schedule of Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00.

**Out of office** — Add date-range blocks when you can't be booked, on the OOO calendar. For
example, block December 24–31.

### Notifications

Choose which events notify you, and on which channels. The page is organized by **channel
tab** — select a tab, then toggle the notification types you want delivered there.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In-app">In-product notifications.</Tab>
  <Tab title="Slack">Pick the Slack channel that receives them. Requires the Slack integration.</Tab>
  <Tab title="Mail">Email notifications. Requires an email on your profile.</Tab>
  <Tab title="SMS">Text notifications. Requires a phone number on your profile.</Tab>
</Tabs>

You can be notified about meetings, workflow errors, form errors, and routing assignments.

<Info>
  Your workspace sets which channels are available and the team defaults. You can turn
  notifications off, but you can't turn on a channel your workspace has disabled — the
  workspace sets the ceiling, and you adjust within it.
</Info>

## Workspace settings

<Info>Workspace settings are available to admins.</Info>

### Users

Manage your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a member">Search by name or email, or filter by role.</Step>
  <Step title="Invite teammates">Invite new members by email.</Step>
  <Step title="Make bulk changes">Select multiple members to act on them at once, such as changing roles together.</Step>
  <Step title="Manage a member">Open a member to edit their profile, availability, integrations, and notifications. The member's **Integrations** tab is where their calendar (Google or Outlook) and conferencing (Zoom) connections live.</Step>
</Steps>

### Workspace notifications

Set org-wide notification defaults and the master channel switches that determine what
members can enable. For example, route all workflow-error notifications to a shared Slack
channel.

### Branding

Set your workspace logo, favicon, brand colors, and fonts.

<Tip>
  Branding is your **workspace identity**. The appearance of your public booking pages is set
  separately under **Scheduling**, below.
</Tip>

### Configurations

- **Objects** — Define the fields on your **Company** and **Person** records, and set a
  priority-ordered list of data sources for each field. For example, fill a company's
  industry from your first provider and fall back to the next.
- **Waterfalls** — Build enrichment chains that try providers in order and accept the first
  result. For example, an email-finder chain of Apollo, then Hunter, then Clearbit.

### Integrations

Connect third-party providers and set workspace defaults.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a provider">Search, or filter the grid by category (CRM, Enrichment, Conferencing).</Step>
  <Step title="Connect it">Authorize the provider, such as Salesforce or HubSpot.</Step>
  <Step title="Set defaults">Choose your workspace's default calendar, conferencing, and enrichment providers.</Step>
</Steps>

### Scheduling

Theme your public booking pages, with a live preview beside the editor.

| Control | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| **Presets** | Starting themes; can seed from your workspace branding. |
| **Background & text** | Page background and text colors. |
| **Buttons** | Button background and text colors. |
| **Fonts** | The booking page's primary font. |
| **Logo** | The logo shown on booking pages, which defaults to your workspace logo. |
| **Default footer** | Show or hide the Default footer. |

When you're happy with the preview, **Publish** to make the theme live.

Set up enterprise sign-in: connect **SSO** and verify your **domains** through the secure
